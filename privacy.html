Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya

Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya ("we", "our", "the University") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all applicants and participants in the AI Certificate Programs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for any of our AI Certificate Programs, we collect the following information:

  • Full name, email address, and mobile number
  • Date of birth, gender, and address
  • Educational qualification and institution name
  • Category (General / OBC / SC / ST / EWS)
  • GGV student status and uploaded ID card (if applicable)
  • Payment transaction reference number and payment screenshot

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Processing and verifying your registration and payment
  • Communicating confirmation, updates, and important notices
  • Issuing certificates upon successful completion of the program
  • Maintaining official academic records for audit and compliance
  • Improving our programs and support services

3. Data Storage and Security

All registration data is stored securely in the University's Google Workspace environment. Uploaded documents (ID cards, payment screenshots) are stored in a restricted Google Drive folder accessible only to authorized program administrators.

🔐 Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Access is limited to the Certificate Program Director and designated verification staff.

4. Data Retention

Registration data and uploaded documents are retained for the duration required by University academic and financial record-keeping policies. After this period, data may be archived or securely deleted.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data after program completion (subject to policy)
  • Withdraw your consent by contacting the program office

6. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that identify individual users. Any technical logs maintained by our hosting provider are used only for security and performance monitoring.

7. Third-Party Services

We use Google Workspace (Sheets, Drive, Gmail) and Google Apps Script to process registrations and send confirmation emails. These services operate under Google's own privacy and security policies, which meet industry standards.

8. Updates to This Policy

The University reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed. Any significant changes will be communicated via the program notice board or email.
