Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about eligibility, certification, and program logistics.

When does the course launch?
All three AI Certification / Internship programs launch on 5 October 2026. Registration is open now.
Who can enroll in these AI Certification / Internship programs?
AI2026A1: 12th-pass students (any stream). AI2026B1: Graduate students from Commerce, Arts, Agriculture, Science, Education, Pharmacy, Management, Civil, Mechanical, Biotechnology, Electrical, Electronics, CS, IT, AI, ML, or DS. AI2026C1: Working professionals, faculty, office staff, entrepreneurs, and managers.
What is the duration and mode of delivery?
Duration is 3–4 months. AI2026A1: 70 contact hours (7h per module). AI2026B1: 80 contact hours (35h theory + 45h practical). AI2026C1: 80 contact hours (8h per module). Classes are held online/offline in blended mode.
What is the attendance requirement?
Minimum 60% attendance is mandatory for examination and certification eligibility. Relaxation requires written approval from the competent authority.
How is assessment done?
Continuous assessment includes assignments, practical tasks, quizzes, and projects. A final certification exam (written/online/practical/viva) is compulsory. Minimum 40% aggregate marks required.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes, certificates are issued by Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya after successful completion of assessments, projects, and attendance requirements. The certificate bears the signature of the Certificate Program Director.
What tools will I learn?
You will get hands-on exposure to ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva AI, Gamma, Tome, AI video/audio tools, no-code platforms, automation systems, and enterprise AI dashboards.
